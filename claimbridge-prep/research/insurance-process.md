# U.S. claim and appeal process: bounded research

## Identify the governing system before choosing a deadline

Record plan year, service date, employer/private versus public coverage, self-funded versus insured, grandfathering, state and administrator. An insurer logo does not establish who bears claim risk. The demo expressly supplies a private single-employer self-funded, non-grandfathered ERISA plan in its synthetic SPD. Medicare and Medicaid are outside MVP routing.

The plan supplies benefits, exclusions, cost-sharing amounts and administrative instructions. Federal rules may constrain those terms. State routes vary; CDI's IMR is a bounded state example (R09), not a route for every California resident. Do not infer a universal state preemption rule from this limited review.

## Workflow

1. Match service and member to eligibility, coverage and authorization scope.
2. A submitted claim receives adjudication reflected in an EOB; a provider separately bills (R12/R13).
3. Compare service-line billed, allowed, paid, adjustment and responsibility amounts; null means unknown, not zero. A balance can reflect a denial rather than deductible.
4. Classify the stated denial and obtain the records needed to challenge it. Correction, internal appeal and provider billing discussion can proceed together; do not assume a correction pauses the appeal clock.
5. Internal appeal: R01 governs this demo's deadline floor and relevant-record request; the synthetic plan adopts one review level. Date of receipt is needed to compute the filing date.
6. External review: R04 covers the chosen federal route and surprise-billing subject matter. Verify exhaustion, final notice, eligibility, destination and deadline. Consumer benefit review is distinct from provider-plan rate IDR and uninsured/self-pay bill disputes.

## Deadline implementation

Store the rule, triggering event, receipt evidence, duration, calendar convention, any exception and calculation together. Never replace 180 days with six calendar months. Before receipt is known display '180 days after receipt; date needed.' After the demo receipt is provided, calculate deterministically. A bill's requested payment date is a different field. Do not automatically calculate an external-review date without the appropriate notice.

R08 contains a time-limited 2026 HHS-process extension. Its presence is a practical freshness warning; this demo does not use that process. Do not reuse cached deadline guidance indefinitely.

## Uncertainty and legal scope

This is source-backed product preparation, not a 50-state legal survey. Actual eligibility, enrollment, facility participation and submitted claims require records. Sources can support a dispute pathway without proving the disputed bill is wrong. Coverage, recognized cost-sharing amount and final provider reimbursement remain separate questions.
