# Competitive check

Verified official product pages on 2026-09-24; capability statements below are vendor descriptions, not hands-on evaluations.

| Alternative | Observed offering | Consequence for ClaimBridge |
|---|---|---|
| Fight Health Insurance (C01) | AI appeal drafting | 'Uses AI to write appeals' is a weak differentiator |
| Counterforce Health (C02) | Document upload, evidence-based appeal letters and voice guidance | Even policy-aware, cited letters are not a unique claim |
| Patient Advocate Foundation (C03) | Human case management, appeals and affordability help for eligible patients | A useful escalation path; software cannot replace clinical or advocacy judgment |

Generic document chat can summarize and cite files. ClaimBridge's proposed advantage must be demonstrated as maintained claim state, explicit contradictory facts, jurisdiction filtering, reproducible calculations, questions that change the analysis, and an actionable packet. These are engineering commitments, not a proven moat. Run matched document-chat comparisons during the hackathon. No unsupported market sizing, savings rate, competitor win rate or HIPAA certification is adopted.

Product hypotheses: consumers may value one-off help; advocates or employers may value workflow tooling. Pricing, distribution, willingness to pay and conflict-of-interest controls remain unvalidated. Do not monetize sensitive claim data.
