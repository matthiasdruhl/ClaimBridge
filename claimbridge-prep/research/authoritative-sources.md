## R01 — 29 CFR 2560.503-1 — Claims procedure

[U.S. DOL / eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XXV/subchapter-G/part-2560/section-2560.503-1) · federal_regulation

Scope: ERISA employee benefit plans; group-health subsections only. Locator: (h)(2)(iii), (h)(3)(i), (i)(2)(iii)(A).

Group-health claimants receive at least 180 days after receipt to appeal; relevant claim records are available free on request. A single-level post-service appeal ordinarily has a 60-day decision limit; two levels and multiemployer exceptions differ.

Use: Protect appeal rights; do not use bill due date as appeal deadline.

Date: No section-specific date recorded; verified current page on access date.

## R02 — 29 CFR 2590.716-5 — Non-emergency surprise bills

[U.S. DOL / eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XXV/subchapter-L/part-2590/subpart-D/section-2590.716-5) · federal_regulation

Scope: Applicable group health plans, including this synthetic ERISA plan. Locator: (a), (b), (c)(1)-(5), (d).

Covered non-emergency services from nonparticipating providers during visits to participating facilities receive specified cost-sharing protections. Cost sharing uses the recognized amount; the provider payment rate is a separate quantity.

Use: Primary plan-side authority for anesthesia dispute.

Date: Plan years beginning on or after 2022-01-01

## R03 — 45 CFR 149.420 — Provider balance billing

[HHS / eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-B/part-149/subpart-E/section-149.420) · federal_regulation

Scope: Nonparticipating providers at participating qualifying facilities under applicable private coverage. Locator: (a), (b)(1)(i), (i).

Providers cannot impose more than protected cost sharing for qualifying covered services. The notice-and-consent exception is unavailable for anesthesiology. Provider notification to the plan about the participating facility is addressed separately.

Use: A generic consent form does not defeat this anesthesia argument.

Date: Services during plan/policy years beginning on or after 2022-01-01

## R04 — 29 CFR 2590.715-2719 — Internal appeals and external review

[U.S. DOL / eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XXV/subchapter-L/part-2590/subpart-C/section-2590.715-2719) · federal_regulation

## R09 — Independent Medical Review Program

Scope: California CDI-regulated insurance; do not assume all California plans. Locator: Eligibility and timing.
