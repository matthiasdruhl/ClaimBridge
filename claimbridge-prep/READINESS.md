# Submission readiness — September 27, 2026

ClaimBridge is ready for a local synthetic-data demonstration within its stated scope.

## Verified

- Upload, persistence, original PDF viewing, duplicate checks, and workspace revisions.
- Provider-backed structured extraction with exact source restoration and validation.
- Bounded network/location and authorization reasoning with explicit unknowns and conflicts.
- Clarification, deterministic regeneration, immutable history, action plans, and editable appeal drafts.
- Readable source citations in the draft, plus copy, plain-text download, save, and reopen.
- Demo Mode restricted to the exact checked-in packet by PDF SHA-256.
- Final provider suite: 12/12 automatic gates, 38.72-second median analysis time.
- Preparation package: 19 probes, 12 visually reviewed PDF pages, and 45 golden assertions.
- Repository check: 34 backend tests and 13 frontend tests, plus lint, typecheck, build, and formatting.
- Manual normal-Chrome verification of citation inspection, original-PDF page opening, copy, download, and saved-draft reopen.

## Boundaries

- Inputs and evaluation cases are synthetic, English, and born-digital.
- There is no OCR, authentication, hosted deployment, clinical reasoning, automated submission, or final-liability calculation.
- Exact quotations prove provenance, not correct interpretation or applicability.
- Human review is required before using any draft, deadline, plan interpretation, or external rule.
- The manual browser path is verified, but there is no automated full-browser suite.
