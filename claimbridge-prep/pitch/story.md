# Pitch story

**Problem:** A medical bill, EOB, denial and benefit document describe different parts of the same event. A person needs to understand which facts matter before deciding what to do.

**Why the process fails:** Even this fictional case separates the service location, network exception, payment record and appeal instructions across several documents. The missing fact can be more useful than another confident paragraph.

**ClaimBridge:** An evidence-backed claim workspace that joins facts to plan terms, checks scoped authoritative sources, asks consequential questions and prepares the next step.

**Live demo:** Avery's $4,200 anesthesia bill. Show the location conflict, obtain corroboration, update the analysis, and produce a reviewable appeal strategy. No promised recovery.

**How it works:** Structured claim state, immutable document evidence, separate plan/external retrieval, deterministic financial/date validation and bounded model assistance. Every important conclusion has support or an explicit missing condition.

**Why it is different:** Not simply document search or automatic letter generation. The proposed value is maintained state, conflict handling and action tracking that can be evaluated. Competitors already offer AI appeals and citations (C01/C02), so execution and measured reliability must earn the distinction.

**Impact:** In the demo, the outcome is a clearer explanation and defensible next steps. Time saved, dollars recovered and real appeal success remain unmeasured. Do not present the synthetic $4,200 as money actually saved.

**Future:** Pilot with advocates on consented, appropriately governed cases; evaluate varied plans and denial types; validate who pays. Broader navigation is future work. No market-size statistic is needed for this pitch.
